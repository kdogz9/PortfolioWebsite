About me

Welcome to my portfolio website! My name is Keisha and I'm currently a 3rd year univeristy student studying games development. My passion is to make small cozy games with a y2k/girly pop theme to bring that feeling of nostalgia back to people again and remind them that the world doesn't have to be so grey! I also love creating social media content and websites.

Projects

Over the course of 3 years I have created 6 small games. These games have been designed to show off some of my personality and art direction whilst also exploring new techniques and mechanics out of my comfort zone.
