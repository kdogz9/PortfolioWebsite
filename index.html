About me

Welcome to my portfolio website! My name is Keisha and I'm currently a 3rd year university student studying games development. My passion is to make small cozy games with a y2k/girly pop theme to bring that feeling of nostalgia back to people again and remind them that the world doesn't have to be so grey! I also love creating social media content and websites.

Projects

Over the course of 3 years I have created 6 small games. These games have been designed to show off some of my personality and art direction whilst also exploring new techniques and mechanics outside of my comfort zone.

Fairy Sky Glade

Fairy Sky Glade is a 2D platform game where you play as a fairy to collect orbs and power ups to progress to the next level. Each level has been procedurally generated.

The game also uses the Open-Meteo API to change the environment and gameplay depending on real-world weather conditions.

Created with: C, Raylib, Open-Meteo API

Play on Itch.io

Lizard Eggsplore

Lizard Eggsplore is a 3D platform game where you play as a rolling lizard moving through an obstacle course to collect eggs for points.

Created with: C#, Unity
